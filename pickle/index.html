Pickle personal assistant

This site describes a private OAuth client used by a personal assistant program. The program runs on a server operated by its owner and sends the owner reminders and travel information through a private chat.

With the owner's consent, the program reads the owner's own Gmail messages in read-only mode to find travel and booking confirmations (flights, hotels) and show the schedule to the owner. It does not send, delete or change email.

The client is not offered to the public. There is no sign-up, no service to join, and no other user. See the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.
